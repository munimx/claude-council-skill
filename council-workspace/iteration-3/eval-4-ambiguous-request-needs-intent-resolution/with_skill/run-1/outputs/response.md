Before the council can deliberate, it needs to know what this refers to: **What is "the new approach", what is the current approach it would replace, and what problem is the change meant to solve?**

Unclear:
- "the new approach": nothing in the request or context identifies what is being proposed
- "what we have": the current approach, system or practice is not identified
- "we": the team, project or domain is not identified

Also useful, if you have it:
- What problem or goal is prompting reconsideration of the current approach
- Measured shortcomings (if any) of the current approach
- Expected benefits of the new approach and whether they have been demonstrated (prototype, benchmark, prior use)
- Migration or switching cost, including effort, downtime and data or compatibility impact

A few lines on each is enough.
